## Graduate Research Assistant | Computer Vision | Deep Learning | Construction AI

Welcome to my GitHub portfolio.

I am currently pursuing a **Master of Science in Global Smart City** at **Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), South Korea**, where I work as a **Graduate Research Assistant** in the **Smart Construction & Intelligent Technology (SCIT) Laboratory**.

My research focuses on developing **Computer Vision** and **Deep Learning** solutions for **construction safety**, with an emphasis on worker behavior analysis, hazard prediction, human–equipment interaction, and intelligent monitoring systems. I am passionate about applying Artificial Intelligence to create safer, smarter, and more efficient construction environments.

My long-term goal is to become a **Computer Vision and AI Researcher**, contributing to intelligent construction systems through advanced perception, multimodal learning, and AI-driven decision support.
